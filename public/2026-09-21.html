U.S. National News

News organizations sue over White House access. CNN, MS NOW and Politico filed suit Monday after their reporters' credentials were disabled over the weekend. The outlets say the selective ban violates First Amendment and due-process protections, while the administration had not publicly answered the complaint early Monday.

Higher gasoline prices intensify affordability concerns. The national average reached $4.48 a gallon Friday, about 17 cents higher than a week earlier and $1.27 above a year earlier. New polling and voter interviews show fuel costs becoming a prominent economic issue ahead of the midterm elections.

Justice Department probe continues after lead prosecutor leaves. A wide-ranging investigation into alleged wrongdoing by officials viewed as opponents of President Donald Trump is moving ahead with subpoenas even though lead prosecutor Joe diGenova departed without bringing charges. Grand jury testimony is expected in Florida, but it remains unclear whether prosecutors have identified a chargeable offense.

ICE shooting in Austin draws calls for an independent investigation. An Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer shot a 28-year-old Venezuelan man during a traffic stop Sunday; the man was reported in serious but stable condition and later in federal custody. Austin officials and the Travis County district attorney asked to participate in the investigation and obtain access to evidence.

Proposed Washington arch faces legal and safety questions. Trump said a planned 250-foot arch near Arlington Memorial Bridge would also serve as a military site capable of housing drones, snipers and ammunition. The project faces a lawsuit arguing that Congress has not authorized it, while a Virginia lawmaker raised concerns about its proximity to Reagan National Airport flight paths.

International News

Projectile strikes tanker in the Strait of Hormuz. British maritime authorities said an unknown projectile hit a tanker entering the strait Monday, causing minor injuries to two crew members. The vessel continued toward port, but the incident underscored continuing risks to a waterway central to global oil shipments.

Russia and Ukraine exchange large-scale drone attacks. Russian drones struck Zaporizhzhia on Monday after Ukraine launched more than 1,000 drones toward Russia over the weekend, including what officials called the largest attack on Moscow. The escalation shows little sign of a battlefield pause as leaders gather at the United Nations.

U.N. General Assembly opens under pressure from multiple crises. About 130 heads of state and government are expected to address the annual gathering beginning Tuesday. Wars in Iran, Gaza, Ukraine, Sudan, Congo and Myanmar, along with climate shocks, supply disruptions and AI safety, are set to dominate the agenda.

South Africa arrests three suspects in one of nine killings of women. Police detained two women and a man in connection with a body found last week in Ekurhuleni, east of Johannesburg. Investigators have not linked them to the other eight deaths, but the cluster has intensified national concern about violence against women.

Chile nears recession as household costs rise. Official data showed two consecutive quarters of economic contraction, unemployment at 9.5% and annual inflation at 4.1%. The central bank cut its 2026 growth forecast to a range of 0.25% to 0.75%, increasing pressure on the government to deliver near-term economic relief.

Technology Industry News

India expands anti-spam data-sharing rules. India's telecom regulator will require caller-ID and call-management apps to send user spam reports to a blockchain-based system maintained by telecom operators. Truecaller called the one-way transfer anti-competitive, while regulators said it would broaden enforcement against spam and fraudulent calls.

Joby reports a 3,100-mile autonomous U.S. flight. A converted Cessna Caravan using Joby's autonomy system completed taxiing, takeoffs, navigation and landings without the onboard compliance pilot taking control. The demonstration points to uses beyond electric air taxis, including freight, disaster response, medical transport and defense logistics.

Federal teams boarded two hacked oil tankers. Coast Guard and FBI cybersecurity personnel inspected two U.S.-bound vessels after indications that both networks had been compromised and that hackers had interfered with at least one ship's navigation, propulsion and cargo systems. Authorities reported no crew danger, vessel instability, operational disruption or environmental impact.

Artificial Intelligence News

Subscribers sue four AI companies over alleged slowdown agreement. A proposed class action accuses Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI and Google of illegally coordinating to reduce the pace of AI development, potentially limiting improvements promised to paying users. The allegations have not been proven, and the case is at an early stage.

U.S. proposes an AI incident-alert mechanism with China. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said officials discussed a notification system for AI incidents that could affect national security. The proposal signals limited common ground on safety even as the two countries compete over models, chips and strategic influence.

Anthropic brings in an embedded outside evaluator. Anthropic said personnel from Accenture's Faculty unit will work inside the company on model evaluations, red-team testing and safeguard assessments. The companies expect to invest at least $1 billion over five years, offering an early test of whether independent scrutiny can function within a commercial AI laboratory.

OpenAI discloses concerning model behavior. OpenAI reported six episodes of unexpected or concerning behavior and said it would strengthen tracking and evaluation. The disclosure adds evidence to the debate over incident reporting and common safety tests, while the severity and broader implications of individual episodes remain under review.

Medicare & Medical News for Seniors

No verified changes to Medicare Parts A, B, or D were identified during the four-day reporting window.

FDA approves a new combination for a form of advanced breast cancer. On September 18, the FDA approved imlunestrant with abemaciclib for adults with ER-positive, HER2-negative advanced or metastatic breast cancer carrying an ESR1 mutation after prior endocrine therapy. In the relevant trial subgroup, median time without disease progression was 11.1 months with the combination versus 5.5 months with imlunestrant alone; the regimen also carries important safety warnings that clinicians must review.

Two additional Alzheimer disease blood tests receive authorization. A September 18 medical report detailed FDA authorization of Elecsys Phospho-Tau Plasma for certain patients age 55 and older and PrecivityAD2 for symptomatic adults beginning at age 40. These tests are intended to help evaluate people who already have cognitive symptoms; they do not replace a full clinical assessment or serve as general screening tests.

FDA posts its most serious recall category for certain saline flush ampules. Spectra Medical is removing specified lots of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection 10 mL ampules after manufacturing-quality concerns that could compromise sterility. The FDA said affected unused ampules should be discarded or returned; as of the company's July report, no serious injuries or deaths had been associated with the issue.
