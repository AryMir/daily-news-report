International News

  • Israeli strikes in Gaza killed at least a dozen people, including police officers, as civilians fled central Gaza after new evacuation warnings and repeated bombardment.
  • U.N. agencies said more than 500 Rohingya are feared dead after two boats carrying refugees from Myanmar and Bangladesh reportedly capsized in the Bay of Bengal during monsoon conditions.

  • Japan's space agency completed the first test flight of its experimental reusable RV-X rocket, which lifted off, hovered, shifted laterally, and landed safely in under a minute.

Artificial Intelligence News

  • Apple sued OpenAI in California federal court, accusing the company of encouraging recruited Apple employees to share confidential information as OpenAI builds hardware for ChatGPT.
  • Meta pulled a feature from its new Muse Image tool after criticism that public Instagram content could be used to create AI-generated images without meeting user expectations on control and privacy.
  • A new AP report said the AI buildout is lifting prices for products such as laptops and memory chips and could add to electricity costs, creating another inflation concern for consumers and the Federal Reserve.
  • More than 200 economists, computer scientists, and AI researchers signed a Stanford-led letter warning that institutions must act now to manage AI-driven job displacement and broader economic disruption.
  • A Meta Oversight Board study said major AI chatbots can mirror government speech restrictions by being less willing to criticize authoritarian leaders, raising concerns about censorship bias in widely used models.
