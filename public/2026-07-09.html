Executive Summary

U.S. National News

  • A federal judge ruled that E. Jean Carroll can collect $5.8 million being held in escrow from Donald Trump after the Supreme Court left intact the jury finding that Trump sexually abused and defamed her; Trump is appealing the release of the funds.
  • The family of Lorenzo Salgado Araujo is demanding an independent investigation after an ICE officer fatally shot him in Houston during an enforcement operation. Federal authorities say he tried to ram an officer, while relatives and immigrant-rights advocates are challenging that account.
  • John Deere agreed to an FTC-backed settlement that will require it to give farmers and independent repair shops access to the same diagnostic and repair tools available to authorized dealers, a significant win for the right-to-repair movement.
  • A new analysis of early Affordable Care Act rate filings shows insurers are seeking a second straight year of double-digit premium increases for 2027, suggesting little relief for many middle-income policyholders.
  • Maine Democrats are scrambling to replace Senate nominee Graham Platner after he said he would withdraw from the race following a sexual assault allegation that he denies, creating new uncertainty in a key battleground contest.

International News

  • The United States launched new airstrikes on Iran early Thursday, and Iran responded with fire affecting Bahrain, Kuwait, and Qatar, escalating tensions around the Persian Gulf and threatening the interim arrangement intended to wind down the war.
  • President Trump said the United States will license Ukraine to produce Patriot air defense systems, a major shift announced at the NATO summit as Kyiv seeks to strengthen air defenses against Russian missile attacks.
  • South Korea’s Supreme Court upheld a seven-year prison sentence for former President Yoon Suk Yeol in the first top-court ruling tied to his 2024 martial law declaration.
  • Australia and India finalized an administrative deal allowing Australian uranium exports to India for peaceful use, ending a long-running stalemate over nuclear-trade safeguards.
  • Authorities in Bangladesh relocated more than 1,000 Rohingya refugees from vulnerable hillside camps after monsoon-driven landslides killed at least 13 people in recent days, including children in Cox’s Bazar.

Technology Industry News

  • SpaceX is getting upbeat calls from major Wall Street banks after its IPO, but investors have been more cautious, with the stock trading around $150 after topping $200 in its first week as attention shifts to execution on Starship and Starlink.
  • Apple has raised prices on Macs and iPads, blaming a memory-chip shortage tied to AI data-center demand. The company said the jump in component costs was severe enough that it could no longer absorb the increases.
  • Nvidia’s AI chip sales in China are stalling as Huawei and other domestic suppliers gain share in a market increasingly shaped by local demand and U.S.-China technology restrictions.
  • Investors are closely watching SK Hynix’s planned roughly $28 billion U.S. stock offering, which AP says is one of the biggest ever and a key test of how much enthusiasm remains in the AI semiconductor trade.

Artificial Intelligence News

  • OpenAI said it has begun rolling out GPT-Live globally in ChatGPT Voice, using a full-duplex voice architecture designed for more natural back-and-forth conversation and interruptions.
  • OpenAI is also running a limited preview of GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna. The company says Sol is its strongest model yet and that broader availability is planned in the coming weeks after a phased release.
  • The Trump administration has lifted restrictions on Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5, which Anthropic says is again widely available. Access to the stronger Mythos 5 model is being restored only for a select group of approved U.S. organizations.
  • An AP and FRONTLINE investigation found that scam compounds are using U.S. technology and AI tools to industrialize fraud, helping criminal networks automate impersonation, outreach, and social-engineering tactics at scale.
