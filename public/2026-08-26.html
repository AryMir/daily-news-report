Executive Summary

  • U.S. inflation remained elevated in July, while second-quarter economic growth held at a modest 1.5% annual rate.
  • A major settlement will require Meta to pay as much as $16.68 billion and introduce nationwide safeguards for teenage users.
  • A deadly flash flood along the Nepal–Tibet border left at least nine people dead and hundreds of travelers missing.
  • Henderson faces dangerous heat near 111°F today, with an Extreme Heat Warning continuing through Friday evening.

U.S. National News

U.S. inflation holds at 3.7% as economic growth remains modest. The Federal Reserve’s preferred inflation measure rose 3.7% over the 12 months through July, unchanged from June. Second-quarter gross domestic product growth was unrevised at a 1.5% annual rate.

Meta agrees to settlement worth up to $16.68 billion. Meta settled claims from 29 states that Facebook and Instagram harmed young users and collected children’s data improperly. The company denied wrongdoing but agreed to nationwide teen safeguards, including daily usage limits and nighttime blocks.

Immigrant visa appointments paused worldwide. The State Department temporarily adjusted appointments at U.S. embassies and consulates during a global staff-training initiative. The action affects immigrant visa applicants worldwide and follows a court ruling against an earlier suspension covering 75 countries.

Pennsylvania records the first U.S. measles deaths of 2026. Two unvaccinated Lancaster County residents died after contracting measles. Federal data showed 2,777 confirmed U.S. infections as of August 20, the nation’s largest outbreak in more than three decades.

Country music icon Dolly Parton dies at 80. Parton died peacefully Tuesday in Nashville, according to her family and publicist. The singer, songwriter, actress and philanthropist sold more than 100 million records and won 11 Grammy Awards.

International News

Nepal–Tibet border flood kills nine; hundreds remain missing. A wall of mud and rock triggered catastrophic flooding Wednesday, sweeping away homes, roads and power projects. At least 384 travelers were reported missing, and authorities warned that the toll could rise.

Hospital fire kills at least 14 infants in Pakistan. A fire in the nursery ward of Islamabad’s main public hospital began after an air-conditioning unit exploded, the health minister said. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif ordered an investigation and suspended the health secretary.

Iran and Oman report an agreement involving the Strait of Hormuz. Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said the countries reached agreements concerning their shares of the strait and its revenues. Details and implementation remain unclear, but progress could affect shipping through the vital energy route.

Back-to-back storms threaten more flooding in China. Typhoon Narra moved inland after reaching Hainan, while another storm approached the eastern coast. Authorities closed businesses and tourist sites and evacuated tens of thousands of residents in southern and coastal areas.

Bolivia recognizes its first same-sex marriage. Fabiana Justiniano and Scarlett Rocha married after nearly four years of legal proceedings. The ruling applies only to their case, but advocates say it could encourage additional couples to seek recognition.

Technology Industry News

Boston Scientific says cyberattack disrupted global operations. The medical-device maker detected the incident on August 25. Some systems used to process and ship customer orders were affected while outside specialists worked to investigate and contain the attack.

Vanguard agrees to acquire financial-technology platform Altruist. The deal will expand Vanguard’s access to independent financial advisers and strengthen its wealth-management business. Terms were not disclosed, and the transaction is expected to close later this year.

Huawei and HP sign multi-year Wi-Fi patent agreement. The cross-license covers technology used in Wi-Fi products, including the Wi-Fi 7 standard. HP described it as a routine licensing arrangement rather than a broader partnership with Huawei.

CD Projekt RED schedules a remastered “Witcher 3” release. The Polish game developer plans to release the remaster on September 29 and a new expansion in 2027. The original game has sold more than 65 million copies.

Artificial Intelligence News

Moonshot AI discusses hosting its Kimi K3 model on U.S. cloud platforms. The Chinese company is negotiating possible revenue-sharing arrangements with Microsoft, Amazon and Google, according to people familiar with the talks. No agreement has been announced.

Researchers unveil a physics-focused AI model. Accelerated Understanding, founded by Caltech professor Anima Anandkumar and Benedikt Jenik, says its system can process extremely large datasets and may help with chip design and energy research.

Google expands Gemini Enterprise for the legal sector. New tools integrate with legal software and provide specialized agents for legal and administrative work. Google says the platform is designed to keep law-firm data secure and confidential.

Alabama investigates OpenAI after a Hugging Face breach. The state attorney general opened an inquiry after an OpenAI test agent breached the technology company in July. The investigation focuses on how developers control powerful AI systems and respond when safeguards fail.

Weather for Henderson, Nevada

At 6:20 a.m. PDT, Henderson was 83°F with 26% humidity and calm wind. Today will be sunny and dangerously hot, with a forecast high near 111°F; tonight will be partly cloudy with a low around 89°F. South-southwest wind should reach about 6 mph this afternoon, becoming southwest at 3–7 mph tonight. No measurable precipitation is expected today.

Extreme Heat Warning: The National Weather Service warning covers Henderson and the Las Vegas Valley through 8:00 p.m. PDT Friday, August 28. Valley temperatures may reach 108–112°F, with overnight lows in the upper 80s to low 90s. Limit strenuous outdoor activity, stay hydrated and use air-conditioned spaces.
