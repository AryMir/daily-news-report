U.S. National News

Federal judge again blocks mail-ballot order. A federal judge ruled against President Donald Trump's executive order seeking to limit mail voting, a decision likely to be appealed. The litigation is important as states prepare voting rules and administration for November's midterm elections.

Iran war reaches a six-month milestone for the United States. The U.S.-Israel war against Iran entered its sixth month, with the administration emphasizing economic pressure on Tehran. The conflict has become a central foreign-policy and budget issue ahead of the midterms.

Attorney general defends Justice Department record. Attorney General Todd Blanche, speaking with reporters during a Puerto Rico trip, said he is leading the department with integrity amid criticism of actions including the prosecution of former FBI Director James Comey. The interview underscores continuing scrutiny of the department's independence.

Election campaigns sharpen their messages after primaries. With primary contests largely concluded, Democrats and Republicans are setting their arguments for November's general election. Polling and interviews highlighted voters' concerns about prices, health care, immigration, and the economy.

Connecticut immigration operation draws local opposition. Immigration arrests rose in Connecticut despite state laws intended to limit cooperation with federal authorities, according to reporting on a recent operation. Local and state representatives said the operation unsettled communities as schools reopened.

International News

Himalayan flood disaster strains Nepal and China rescue effort. Rescuers in Nepal and neighboring China continued searching after catastrophic flash floods and landslides along their border killed hundreds and left many people missing. Helicopters and ground teams are trying to reach cut-off communities.

Norway's King Harald V dies; Haakon becomes king. King Harald V died at age 89, and his son, Crown Prince Haakon, succeeded him as King Haakon VIII. The transition begins during a difficult period for the Norwegian royal family.

Russian drones hit Ukraine's Kyiv region for a second day. Ukrainian officials said Russian drone attacks struck apartment buildings and warehouses in the Kyiv region. The renewed barrage comes amid continued pressure on civilian infrastructure and emergency services.

South Korea, Japan and the United States plan trilateral drill. Seoul said the three countries will hold a joint military exercise next month in response to North Korean nuclear threats. North Korea has objected to the planned activity and said it would respond to U.S. pressure.

Eswatini receives more U.S. deportees under third-country arrangement. Authorities in Eswatini said two additional migrants deported from the United States arrived under a bilateral arrangement that has drawn criticism from rights groups. The case reflects the wider use of third-country agreements in U.S. immigration policy.

Technology Industry News

Nvidia's quarterly results exceed expectations. Nvidia reported profit and revenue above analysts' forecasts and issued a stronger-than-expected revenue outlook. The results signaled continued demand for advanced chips used in data centers.

Salesforce joins the earnings-led software rebound. Salesforce was among technology companies reporting stronger spring profits, helping lift software shares. Its results were part of a broader session in which investors reassessed growth prospects across the sector.

Technology sector drives Thursday's market advance. The S&P 500 technology sector rose 3.4%, the only major sector gain reported in the session. The concentration of gains highlighted the market's continued sensitivity to large technology companies' earnings and forecasts.

Artificial Intelligence News

Court blocks Pentagon action against Anthropic. A federal judge ruled that the government's designation of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk was unlawful, barring the challenged measures. The case centers on a dispute over limits on military uses of AI systems.

EU transparency rules begin taking effect. A major implementation deadline for the European Union's AI Act arrived this month, bringing greater focus to transparency requirements for AI providers. Anthropic has said future Claude models will generate watermarked text to support compliance.

More than 100 organizations warn about AI-enabled cyberattacks. OpenAI, Anthropic, cloud providers, and other companies joined a call for faster defensive preparation against AI-enabled cyber threats. The statement urges businesses and governments to improve defenses and share threat information.

Anthropic previews a standard for agents operating physical equipment. Anthropic announced a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard, a proposed shared specification for AI agents to operate physical devices. The initial program is aimed at scientific research labs and advanced manufacturers, with safety evaluations as a stated focus.
