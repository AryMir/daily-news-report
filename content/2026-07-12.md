## U.S. National News

- **Sen. Lindsey Graham dies at 71.** Graham, a longtime South Carolina Republican and close Trump ally, died after a brief illness, and Gov. Henry McMaster is expected to appoint a temporary replacement ahead of a special election.

- **Dangerous heat wave expands across much of the country.** Forecasters say a broad heat dome could affect up to two-thirds of the continental U.S., with many areas running 15 to 25 degrees above normal and more than 90 local temperature records at risk.

- **Wildfire triggers evacuations in Los Angeles County's high desert.** A fast-moving fire in northeastern Los Angeles County grew to about 2,700 acres by Friday evening, prompting evacuation orders and a Red Cross shelter as very hot, dry conditions worsened fire danger.

- **New York City's Legionnaires' outbreak widens scrutiny of cooling towers.** City officials said 31 Upper East Side buildings, including the Guggenheim Museum, tested positive for Legionella bacteria; more than 50 people have been affected, fewer than 20 remain hospitalized, and no deaths were reported.

- **Justice Department subpoenas New York Times reporters over Air Force One stories.** Four Times journalists were subpoenaed after reporting on security concerns tied to the retrofitted Qatari-gifted Air Force One, prompting renewed press-freedom criticism.

## International News

- **U.S. strikes Iran after attack on ship in the Strait of Hormuz.** AP reports the U.S. hit 140 Iranian targets after a Cyprus-flagged container ship was struck; Iran then launched retaliatory attacks toward Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, and Jordan.

- **Russian attacks kill six and wound 29 in Ukraine.** Overnight missile and drone strikes hit Kyiv and other areas, while Ukrainian forces continued targeting Russian oil tankers and refinery infrastructure.

- **Deadly wildfire in southern Spain highlights Europe's heat risk.** One of Spain's deadliest wildfires in recent years killed at least 12 people in Almeria province as extreme heat and dry conditions fueled the blaze.

- **Vietnam detains captain after tourist speedboat capsizes.** Vietnamese police detained the captain of a speedboat that overturned off Phu Quoc, killing 15 Indian tourists during a company trip.

- **Typhoon Bavi weakens after landfall but still disrupts eastern China.** Chinese authorities evacuated more than 2 million people as the storm brought strong winds, heavy rain, and widespread flight cancellations after coming ashore in Zhejiang.
