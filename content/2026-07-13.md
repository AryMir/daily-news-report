## U.S. National News

- The Senate returned to Washington under an uncertain agenda after Sen. Lindsey Graham’s death. South Carolina Gov. Henry McMaster must appoint a temporary replacement, and the state is preparing a special Republican primary on Aug. 11.
- Sen. Mitch McConnell said a fall led to his hospitalization, ending weeks of speculation about his health. The Kentucky Republican made the disclosure Sunday as questions continue about his role in a narrowly divided Senate.
- The Justice Department subpoenaed New York Times journalists over reporting on the Qatari-gifted Air Force One, prompting renewed criticism from press-freedom advocates. The AP described it as a significant escalation in the Trump administration’s conflict with the media.
- Residents in Philadelphia-area neighborhoods spent Sunday cleaning up after a string of microburst storms. AP reported downed trees and power lines, flooded streets, and at least one building collapse that crushed cars and blocked trolley tracks.
- A dangerous heat wave is still gripping large parts of the country. AP said temperatures 15 to 25 degrees above normal could affect up to two-thirds of the continental U.S., with especially risky overnight heat in parts of the South and Southwest.

## International News

- The U.S. military said it struck about 140 targets in Iran after Tehran’s attacks in Gulf states and near the Strait of Hormuz. The new round of fighting deepened fears about shipping disruptions through one of the world’s most important energy chokepoints.
- A fire at a Bangkok music bar killed at least 27 people and critically injured 25 others. Thai police said many victims were found trapped in windowless bathrooms near a rear exit.
- Fourteen countries and the European Union reaffirmed the 2016 international ruling that found no legal basis for China’s sweeping South China Sea claims. Their joint statements also rejected destabilizing actions in the disputed waters.
- Typhoon Bavi weakened after reaching China’s eastern coast, but it still brought heavy rain, strong winds and large waves to Zhejiang and surrounding areas. Flooded roads and storm-surge damage were reported as the system moved inland.
- Fifteen Indian tourists killed in a Vietnam speedboat accident were flown home Monday, and authorities continue investigating the captain. The accident has renewed scrutiny of tourist-boat safety near Phu Quoc.

- Meta appealed the Los Angeles verdict that found Instagram and Facebook were designed to hook young users without sufficient regard for their well-being. The case remains one of the most closely watched legal fights over social-media product design.
