- **Missouri seeks to restore new congressional map.** Missouri appealed to the U.S. Supreme Court to permit its new congressional districts for the midterms. - **Pentagon limits advertising tracking on military devices.** The Defense Department disabled advertising tracking on government-issued phones and computers used by the armed forces, responding to concerns that commercially traded location data could be used to target personnel.
