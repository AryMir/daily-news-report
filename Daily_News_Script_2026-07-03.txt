Important:
Use live web browsing/search. Do not rely on memory. Do not invent or estimate current facts. Every major news item, market number, and weather forecast must be checked against current sources.

Critical accuracy rules:

* Do not use generic headlines.
* Do not use old sample information.
* Do not include vague items like “leaders met” or “a report was released” unless you can identify the real event, date, source, and specific details.
* For stock market data, include the actual latest index level, move, and date/time or market session.
* If a market is closed, say “latest available close” and give the date.
* If you cannot verify a fact, leave it out.
* If current web access is unavailable, stop and say: “I cannot create a reliable current Daily News Report without live web access.”

Report format:
Write in clean Markdown, not HTML.

Title:

# Daily News Report | [Today’s Date]

Sections:

## Executive Summary

Write 3–5 short bullets summarizing the most important developments.

## U.S. National News

Provide 5 verified headlines. Each item should be 1–2 sentences.

## International News

Provide 5 verified headlines. Each item should be 1–2 sentences.

## Stock Market News

Create a simple table with these columns:

Index / Asset | Latest Move | Latest Level | Main Driver | Source/Time

Include:

* Dow Jones Industrial Average
* S&P 500
* Nasdaq Composite
* Brent or WTI crude oil

After the table, write 2–3 sentences explaining the main market drivers.

## Technology Industry News

Provide 3–5 verified items. Each item should be 1–2 sentences.

## Artificial Intelligence News

Provide 3–5 verified items. Each item should be 1–2 sentences.

## Weather for [Current Location from config.json]

Include:

* Current or latest temperature
* Today’s high and low
* Conditions
* Wind if relevant
* Precipitation chance
* Weather alerts or heat concerns if any

## Sources Checked

List the main sources used, grouped by section. Include source names and links.

Style requirements:

* Be factual, calm, and neutral.
* Avoid hype and political opinion.
* Use clear language.
* Keep each bullet concise.
* Do not include filler.
* Do not apologize unless live web access is unavailable.
* Do not make predictions beyond what sources report.

Final output instructions:

* Output only the finished Markdown report.
* Do not put the report inside a code block.
* Do not add explanations before or after the report.
